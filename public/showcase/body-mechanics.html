Body Mechanics

Real-time 3D skeleton viewer for walking gait & breathing education

An interactive, browser-native biomechanics visualizer that animates a full human skeleton through procedural gait and breathing cycles — with kinetic chain highlighting, overlay controls, and A/B preset comparison. No plugins. No install. Just npm run dev.

React 18 react-three-fiber Three.js Zustand Vite + Vitest Static Deploy

Overview

What it does & who it's for

Body Mechanics renders an anatomically-structured skeleton in 3D and animates it through two biomechanical modules: a walking gait cycle (with 10 configurable parameters — hip swing, knee flexion, ankle range, overpronation, pelvic lateral sway, etc.) and a breathing cycle (diaphragmatic vs. chest breathing, adjustable BPM).

The target audience includes physical therapists, biomechanics educators, movement coaches, and allied health students who need a lightweight visual reference that runs in any browser without clinical software licenses.

The problem it solves: explaining joint compensation patterns to patients or students is difficult without a live, controllable reference. Existing tools either require Unity installs or are locked behind expensive clinical platforms.

Feature Highlights

Core capabilities

🦴

Procedural FK Animation

Walking and breathing animations are computed as pure functions of time and parameters — no pre-baked clips. Every preset is just a different parameter set fed into the same FK pipeline.

🕸

Kinetic Chain Graph Traversal

The skeleton is an undirected adjacency graph. Clicking any joint triggers BFS to highlight the upstream path to root and downstream subtree — showing how forces propagate through the chain.

🔀

A/B Preset Comparison

Side-by-side split view renders two different gait presets simultaneously (e.g. normal vs. overpronation), making compensation patterns immediately visible at a glance.

📊

Physics Overlays

Toggle-able overlays include: Centre of Pressure trail, Ground Reaction Force arrows, Rib Expansion directional arrows, and Joint Axes RGB triads — each driven by the animation data pipeline.

🎛

Configurable Gait Presets

Presets (normal, overpronation, limited dorsiflexion, chest breathing, diaphragmatic) expose 10+ biomechanical parameters, with gait phase callouts synchronized to the timeline scrubber.

🧪

Tested Graph Module

The graph traversal layer (BFS, DFS, path-finding, highlight set) is fully unit-tested with Vitest — 11 tests ensuring correctness of joint chain logic independent of Three.js rendering.

Why It Matters

Technical depth & practical value

The architecture deliberately separates concerns into three independent layers: pure-math animation functions, a graph traversal module, and React/Three.js rendering. This means the animation logic is fully testable without a browser, the graph logic is portable, and the rendering layer could be swapped for Unity or a real GLTF rig without touching the data model.

Pre-allocated THREE.js objects at module scope and a material cache prevent garbage collection pressure inside the render loop — a common source of frame drops in browser 3D. All animation values are computed each frame from a normalized time parameter, so scrubbing is instant and deterministic.
