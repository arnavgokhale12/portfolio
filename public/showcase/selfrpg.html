SelfRPG

Your life is the game. Complete quests. Level up. Don't let your stats decay.

A React Native habit tracker built as an RPG: complete daily quests, earn XP and coins, build a character across 8 life attributes with daily decay mechanics, unlock talents, and choose a build archetype. Your real-world habits are the gameplay.

Overview

What it does & who it's for

SelfRPG maps real-life habits onto RPG mechanics: you have a character with 8 attributes (Vitality, Strength, Mind, Focus, Mood, Social, Order, Discipline), each with a current value (0–100), a daily decay rate, and a floor. If you don't complete quests that feed an attribute, its value drops toward the floor — just like a character in an RPG who hasn't trained.

A Quest Engine generates a daily board of habit quests from a curated pool, each with attribute effects, XP rewards, and coin rewards. Completing quests levels up your character, earns skill points for the Upgrade Tree, and triggers achievement unlocks. A Stats Engine applies daily decay on day rollover and computes status effects (buffs/debuffs) based on current attribute levels — low Focus gives "Brain Fog" (-10% XP), high Vitality gives "Well Rested" (+15% XP).

Built for people who find traditional habit trackers too austere. The RPG framing converts abstract self-improvement goals into concrete daily quests with visible stat consequences.

Feature Highlights

Core capabilities

⚔️

Quest Engine

A curated quest pool spans all 8 attributes with daily, weekly, one-off, and challenge types. Each quest specifies attribute effects, XP reward, coin reward, difficulty (1–5), and estimated duration. The Quest Engine populates each day's board deterministically.

📉

Daily Attribute Decay

The Stats Engine applies per-attribute decay rates on day rollover, clamped to each attribute's floor. Neglect an attribute for a few days and the stat consequences are visible — motivating consistent habit completion.

✨

Status Effects

Attribute levels trigger dynamic buffs and debuffs: "Brain Fog" (Focus <30, −10% XP), "Scattered" (Focus <15), "Well Rested" (Vitality >70, +15% XP), "In Flow" (Mind + Focus both high). Status effects affect XP gain and gameplay feel.

🌳

Upgrade Tree

Skill points earned from leveling up can be spent in the Talent Upgrade Tree. Talents modify game mechanics — reducing decay rates, boosting XP multipliers, or unlocking passive bonuses tied to build archetypes.

🏗

Build Templates

Choose a character archetype at start: Scholar, Athlete, Social Charisma, Order & Productivity, or Custom. Each template pre-configures attribute priorities, decay rates, and starting talent suggestions.

🏆

Achievements & History

Four achievement categories (milestone, streak, attribute, special) with XP and coin rewards. A history screen tracks completed quests over time, letting users review their consistency across weeks.

Why It Matters

Design thinking & technical execution

The central design insight is that habit trackers fail because they're boring. By mapping behavior change onto RPG mechanics — daily decay, status effects, level-up progression, talent trees — SelfRPG makes the consequences of habits concrete and visible, and the rewards immediate and satisfying.

The technical execution demonstrates a well-structured React Native app: strict TypeScript interfaces for every game entity (Quest, Attribute, Character, Achievement, Talent), a clean service layer (QuestEngine, StatsEngine, SchedulerService) separate from the UI, Zustand for game state, and expo-sqlite for persistent storage. The day rollover service handles edge cases like multi-day gaps in app usage (applying accumulated decay correctly).
